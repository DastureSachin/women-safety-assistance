Practical Safety Tips

Simple reminders for planning ahead and finding support.

  • Share your plans and expected arrival time with someone you trust.
  • Keep your phone charged and save local emergency numbers.
  • Choose well-lit, familiar routes when possible and trust your instincts.
  • If you feel unsafe, move toward a staffed or public place and contact someone you trust.
  • Review your trusted contacts and make sure their phone numbers are current.
  • For immediate danger, contact local emergency services directly. This demo does not do that for you.
